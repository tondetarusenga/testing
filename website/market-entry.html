The questions it answers

Before you launch.

01

Is the demand real here?

Bottoms-up sizing for this city, not a national headline number. Johannesburg is not Kimberley.

02

How will incumbents respond?

Ignore, copy, price war or acquire, and what each would cost you.

03

Can you win, and on what?

Whether your advantage is a real moat or a speed bump competitors can match.

04

Do the economics work?

Pricing, take rate, contribution per order and the capital needed, including a price-war scenario.
