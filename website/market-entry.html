The method

Four gates, one decision at each.

The memo covers Gates 1 and 2. Pricing, economics and launch (Gates 3 and 4) can follow if the answer is go.

GATE 1

Should we enter?

  • Is there proven demand, with real people willing to pay?
  • What is the realistic year-one market, not the TAM?
  • Is the market greenfield, crowded or ready to consolidate?
GATE 2

Can we win?

  • Is there an advantage that can't be copied in 6–12 months?
  • Can you beat incumbents on their weakest point?
  • Will supply and demand dynamics favour you once you scale?
GATE 3

Can we afford it?

  • What capital reaches critical mass before the flywheel works?
  • Do unit economics hold at scale, after incentives, refunds and fulfilment?
  • Can you outlast a price war?
GATE 4

How do we execute?

  • Critical mass on supply and demand at once.
  • Unit economics improving in real time.
  • Operating systems that don't grow with headcount.
Hard stops

Five reasons the answer is no.

If any of these is true, don't enter. Most failed entries ignored at least one.

  • You're creating demand from scratch.
  • A well-funded incumbent with network effects owns the market, and you have no structural moat.
  • The regulatory environment is hostile or unclear.
  • Year-one addressable revenue is under US$100,000.
  • You haven't talked to real customers. Friends and surveys don't count.

FAQ

Questions.

Which markets?

South Africa and Zimbabwe first-hand, plus wider Southern Africa. If you're looking further afield, I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right fit.

What if you advise us not to enter?

Then the memo has paid for itself. A clear no, with reasons, costs far less than a launch that fails.

Do you help with the launch itself?

Yes. After the memo, I can support the first 90 days: supply acquisition, first transactions and the operating metrics.

We're already in market. Is this for us?

An entry memo is for new markets. If you're already operating, a pricing review, supply and incentive audit or liquidity diagnosis is likely a better fit.

What do you need from us?

Your business model, any metrics from other markets, and a decision-maker for the scoping and debrief calls.
