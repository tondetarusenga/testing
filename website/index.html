The gap

The deck says one thing. The operator checks another.

What the deck saysWhat an operator checks
"GMV is up 40% month on month."How much of it is bought with incentives, and what survives when they stop.
"The market is huge."The size of this city, not the country. Johannesburg is not Kimberley.
"We have a network effect."Whether it is real, or a feature a competitor can copy in a quarter.
"Retention is strong."Whether new-user growth is hiding churn, and whether trade is leaving the app.
