How I work

Four questions, asked in order.

I stop at the first one where the answer is no. Plenty of plans don't get past the first.

1

Should you enter?

  • Are people already paying for this, or would you have to create the demand?
  • How much can you realistically earn in year one, in this city?
2

Can you win?

  • What do you have that the incumbent couldn't copy within a year?
  • How will they respond: ignore you, copy you, cut prices or buy you?
3

Can you afford it?

  • What is left from each order after payments, refunds, incentives and delivery?
  • Could you survive six months of a price war?
4

How will you run it?

  • Supply first, then demand. How do you get the first 50 to 100 orders?
  • Which numbers do you watch each week?
