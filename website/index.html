The gap in marketplace diligence

A deck can show GMV, growth and a big market. It rarely shows whether orders are matched, whether suppliers stay without incentives, or whether trade is moving off the app.

Most funds don't have an operator on the team who has run these numbers in African cities. This is that second opinion, scoped to one company and one decision.

The operator memo

One target company, delivered in about 7–10 days, 10–15 pages. It covers:

01

Liquidity and unit economics

Fill rate, utilisation and contribution per order, including a stress test with subsidies removed.

02

Pricing and take rate

Whether the pricing holds against incumbents and local competitors.

03

Competitive position

How an incumbent is likely to respond, and whether the claimed moat is real.

04

Retention and leakage

Whether growth is masking churn, and whether trade is leaving the platform.

05

Verdict and questions

A clear view, plus the questions to put to the founders.

A faster first-look screen is also available when you need a view before committing to the full memo.
