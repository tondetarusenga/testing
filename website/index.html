The framework

Four gates. Each one a go or no-go.

Every engagement runs on the same four checkpoints. You can stop at any gate, and often should.

GATE 1

Should we enter?

  • Is there proven demand, not just a good idea?
  • What is the realistic year-one market, not the TAM?
  • Greenfield, crowded or ready to consolidate?
GATE 2

Can we win?

  • Is there an advantage competitors can't copy in 6–12 months?
  • Can you beat incumbents on their weakest point?
  • Does supply and demand favour you at scale?
GATE 3

Can we afford it?

  • How much capital to reach critical mass?
  • Do the unit economics work at scale?
  • Can you outlast a price war?
GATE 4

How do we execute?

  • Critical mass on both sides at once?
  • Unit economics improving in real time?
  • Systems that don't scale with headcount?
