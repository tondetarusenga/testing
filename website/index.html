FAQ

Questions.

How does the Entry Decision work?

Four to six weeks. A kickoff to agree the market and the question, a weekly check-in, then a written memo and a debrief. You leave with a go or no-go, a competitor map, a pricing model and a 90-day plan.

How quickly can we start?

Usually within a week of agreeing scope. We keep only a few projects running at once.

How do you charge?

A fixed fee per project, agreed up front. Entry Decisions start from US$5,000. Investor Diligence is quoted per deal. No hourly billing.

Which markets do you cover?

South Africa, Zimbabwe and the wider Southern African region.

What if we're not ready for a full project?

Book a free 20-minute call. We'll tell you honestly whether we can help, and if not, who can.
