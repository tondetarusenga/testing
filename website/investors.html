What's left once the incentives stop?

An operator's read on a marketplace before you invest. Seven to ten days.

Get in touch about a deal
What we check

The things a deck tends to skip.

The deck saysWhat we check
"GMV is up 40% month on month."How much of that growth is paid for with incentives, and what each order earns without them.
"The market is huge."The realistic market in the places they actually operate.
"Strong network effects."Whether a competitor could match it within a year.
"Retention is strong."Repeat rates by month of joining, so new users aren't hiding the ones who left.
"Supply is growing."How busy suppliers are, and how many leave when incentives drop.
What you get

A written memo and a call.

  • Our view on the operating riskPlainly stated, with the evidence behind it.
  • The numbers per orderWith and without incentives, from the data room.
  • Competition and pricingWhether their prices hold up against the incumbents and local competitors.
  • Questions for the foundersThe ones we'd want answered before signing.

Usually 7 to 10 days, depending on the data room. Quoted per deal. A shorter first look is possible if you need a view quickly.
